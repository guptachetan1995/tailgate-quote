The problem

The job is in the owner's head at the van. The quote waits until evening.

Quoted from memory

A small plumbing or HVAC firm's owner walks the job, then drives to the next one. The details live in his head until he types them up.

Typed late

A quote typed at night takes 20 to 34 minutes by QuoteIQ's own published figure, and the customer waits a day for a price.

Details slip

Between the driveway and the keyboard, a valve size or a length of pipe gets lost, and the quote is wrong before it is sent.

The solution

Say the job once. Answer one question. Find a correct draft on your phone.

Talk

The owner talks the job through at the tailgate. The agent listens, asks when something is ambiguous ("half-inch or three-quarter?") and reads the draft back.

Draft

Every line cites the owner's exact words. Prices come only from his price list. A line whose words, quantity or customer don't match what was said is refused.

Decide

Sending is a tap, never a sentence. A voice anyone nearby can fake is not a signature, so "go ahead and send it" is heard, logged and refused.
